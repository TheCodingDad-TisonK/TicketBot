## Tips for Best Results

1. **Use the square format** - Discord avatars are displayed as circles, so keep important elements centered
2. **Request transparent background** - Most AI generators support this with "transparent background" or "PNG with alpha"
3. **Specify "Discord bot avatar"** - This helps the AI understand the intended use
4. **Keep it simple** - Complex designs don't work well at small sizes
5. **High contrast** - Ensure the design is visible at 128x128 and 64x64 sizes

---

## Recommended AI Tools to Use

- **Midjourney** (Discord) - `/imagine prompt: [your chosen prompt]`
- **DALL-E 3** (ChatGPT) - "Generate an image with this prompt..."
- **Stable Diffusion** - Use with "transparent background" or "PNG" in prompt
- **Bing Image Creator** - Free, uses DALL-E 3
- **Canva AI** - Also has AI image generation features

---

*After generating, crop/center the image to 512x512 and convert to PNG for best Discord compatibility.*
